# Loyalty Rewards Program

Northwind Outfitters Rewards is our free customer loyalty program designed to reward repeat shoppers.

---

## Membership

Joining the loyalty program is free.

Customers can enroll by:

- Creating an online account
- Registering in-store
- Downloading the Northwind Outfitters mobile app

Members earn points on every eligible purchase.

---

## Membership Levels

### Silver

Requirements

- Free upon registration

Benefits

- Earn 1 point for every $1 spent
- Birthday reward
- Early access to seasonal sales

---

### Gold

Requirements

- Spend $500 within a calendar year

Benefits

- Earn 1.25 points per $1 spent
- Free standard shipping
- Priority customer support
- Exclusive product launches

---

### Platinum

Requirements

- Spend $1,500 within a calendar year

Benefits

- Earn 1.5 points per $1 spent
- Free express shipping
- Personal shopping assistance
- Invitations to VIP events
- Complimentary alterations on eligible apparel

---

## Redeeming Points

100 points = $5 reward.

Rewards can be redeemed during checkout both online and in stores.

Points cannot be exchanged for cash.

---

## Expiration

Points expire after 12 months of account inactivity.

Making any purchase resets the expiration period.

---

## Exclusions

Points are not earned on:

- Gift card purchases
- Taxes
- Shipping charges
- Returned merchandise

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is membership free?

Yes. There is no fee to join.

Q: Can I transfer points?

No. Points are non-transferable.

Q: Can rewards be combined?

Yes. Rewards may be combined with eligible promotions unless otherwise stated.
