# Gift Cards

Northwind Outfitters Gift Cards are available in both physical and digital formats.

---

## Available Values

Gift cards may be purchased in the following amounts:

- $25
- $50
- $100
- $250
- $500

---

## Delivery

Digital gift cards are delivered by email within a few minutes after purchase.

Physical gift cards are shipped using standard delivery.

---

## Redemption

Gift cards may be redeemed:

- Online
- In retail stores
- Through the mobile app

---

## Expiration

Northwind Outfitters Gift Cards never expire.

---

## Balance

Customers may check their remaining balance:

- Online
- In stores
- By contacting customer support

---

## Reloading

Gift cards cannot currently be reloaded.

---

## Refunds

Gift cards are non-refundable after purchase.

Lost or stolen cards cannot be replaced unless proof of purchase is available.

---

## Restrictions

Gift cards cannot be used to purchase additional gift cards.

Gift cards cannot be exchanged for cash except where required by law.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do gift cards expire?

No. They never expire.

Q: Can I use multiple gift cards?

Yes. Multiple gift cards may be used for a single purchase.

Q: Can I combine a gift card with a credit card?

Yes. Remaining balances may be paid using another payment method.
