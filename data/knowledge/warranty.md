# Product Warranty

Northwind Outfitters stands behind the quality of every product we sell.

---

## Manufacturing Defects

Products with manufacturing defects are covered for one year from the date of purchase.

Examples include:

- Broken stitching
- Defective zippers
- Faulty buttons
- Fabric manufacturing defects

---

## Not Covered

The warranty does not cover:

- Normal wear and tear
- Accidental damage
- Improper washing
- Alterations performed by third parties
- Damage caused by misuse

---

## Warranty Claims

Customers should contact customer support with:

- Order number
- Photos of the defect
- Product description

Claims are usually reviewed within three business days.

---

## Resolution Options

Approved warranty claims may receive:

- Product replacement
- Repair
- Store credit
- Refund when replacement is unavailable

The appropriate resolution is determined after inspection.

---

## International Warranty

Warranty coverage applies worldwide for products purchased directly from Northwind Outfitters.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does the warranty cover fading?

No. Normal fading caused by washing or sunlight is not covered.

Q: Is accidental damage covered?

No.

Q: Is proof of purchase required?

Yes. Customers must provide a valid receipt or order number.
