# Promotions and Discounts

Northwind Outfitters regularly offers promotional discounts throughout the year.

---

## Seasonal Sales

Major promotions include:

- Spring Sale
- Summer Clearance
- Back-to-School Event
- Black Friday
- Cyber Monday
- Holiday Sale

Discounts vary by promotion.

---

## Coupon Codes

Coupon codes may provide:

- Percentage discounts
- Fixed dollar discounts
- Free shipping
- Bonus loyalty points

Coupons must be entered during checkout.

---

## Coupon Rules

Only one coupon code may be applied per order unless explicitly stated.

Coupons cannot be applied after an order has been placed.

Coupons cannot be exchanged for cash.

---

## Student Discount

Verified students receive 10% off eligible products.

Verification is performed through our student verification partner.

---

## Military Discount

Active-duty military personnel and veterans receive 10% off eligible purchases.

Proof of service is required.

---

## First Order Discount

New customers receive 15% off their first purchase after subscribing to our newsletter.

---

## Price Matching

Northwind Outfitters does not currently offer price matching.

---

## Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I combine multiple coupons?

No. Only one coupon may be applied unless specifically mentioned.

Q: Do promotions apply to gift cards?

No.

Q: Are clearance items eligible?

Most clearance products are excluded unless the promotion specifically includes them.
